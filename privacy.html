Callout – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Midwest Solutions LLC

This policy covers the Callout: Weld Symbol Trainer app for Android (package com.calloutweld.app), published by Midwest Solutions LLC ("we", "us").

Summary: the app does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for your email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It requests no Android permissions and does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

So that it can show your progress, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device only:

  • the lessons you have completed, your quiz and exam results, the questions you missed and the days you studied
  • your bookmarks and your light or dark theme choice
  • the name you type for your certificate of completion, if you type one
  • that you have read the terms and disclaimers

Deleting it

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved progress in your personal Google backup so that it can be restored on a new device. That backup is run by Google under your Google account and is not accessible to us.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software and shares no data with anyone. It is distributed through Google Play. Google handles your purchase, download and account information under Google's own privacy policy, and we do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is a technical training tool intended for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and its effective date changed before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sales@midtekpower.com. Midwest Solutions LLC.
