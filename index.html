Callout teaches you to read a welding symbol, picture the weld it calls for, and check the finished job against it. It starts from zero and works up to full fabrication drawings, in short lessons of a few minutes each. It is built for welders, fitters, fabricators, inspectors, engineers, supervisors and students.

What's inside

  • A complete course14 modules and 92 short lessons, from "what is a welding symbol" to reading whole drawings. Every lesson ends with a knowledge check.
  • Symbols drawn liveEvery welding symbol is drawn from its data and explained in plain English, element by element, next to a cross-section of the joint.
  • Symbol builderPick the weld, side, size, length, pitch, groove angle, root opening, contour and tail, and get a checked symbol with its reading. Fillet welds and seven groove types.
  • Hands-on labsArrow side and other side, intermittent welds, groove preparation, and a library of the five basic joints.
  • Practice drawings12 original fabrication drawings, from a single tab to a vessel nozzle, with numbered symbols to read and check.
  • Questions and examsNearly 400 original questions with explanations, daily practice, missed-question review, 10 timed practice exams and a final assessment.
  • Reference libraryMore than 150 searchable entries for symbols, dimensions, joints, terms, processes and examination methods, with bookmarks and flashcards.
  • Calculators13 shop calculators, each showing its formula and assumptions: throat and leg, weld volume and weight, filler metal, arc time, cost, heat input and more.
  • Progress you can seeProgress bars for the course and every module, accuracy by topic, study streaks, exam scores and a certificate of course completion.
